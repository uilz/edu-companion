# Domain: Learner

> **Core question: How does AppleGo understand the learner?**
>
> Objects that appear here: Profile, Learner Model
> Parent domain: [Environment](./environment.md)

---

## What AppleGo Knows

AppleGo builds understanding of the learner over time. This understanding is not a profile form. It is accumulated observation.

### Learning Style

How the learner learns best.

- "Prefers visual explanations before formal ones."
- "Draws everything first. Then derives."
- "Learns by building. Projects accelerate their understanding."
- "Needs to talk through concepts to internalize them."

These patterns emerge over weeks. They cannot be asked in a questionnaire — the learner may not know themselves.

### Pace and Rhythm

The learner's natural tempo.

- Typical session length
- When they tend to pause or rest
- How quickly they move from intake to testing
- Whether they like to go deep on one concept or explore broadly

### Strengths and Struggles

Where the learner naturally excels and where they consistently face resistance.

- "Quick with visual concepts. Slower with symbolic notation."
- "Excels at applied problems. Struggles with abstract theory."
- "Never gives up. Works through confusion systematically."

### Current State

The learner's state right now.

- Current energy: engaged, tired, distracted, in flow
- Current understanding of the active concept
- Current emotional state: frustrated, curious, confident, tentative
- What they were doing when they last left

### Learning History

The full arc.

- What they've learned
- How their understanding evolved
- When they struggled and when they broke through
- What they've built
- How they've grown

## What AppleGo Does Not Know

Equally important: what AppleGo deliberately does not track.

- **Identity data.** No demographics, no location, no personal details beyond what the learner explicitly shares in conversation.
- **External performance.** No grades, no test scores, no institutional assessments. AppleGo understands the learner through their learning — not through third-party metrics.
- **Private thoughts.** What the learner thinks but does not express. AppleGo observes behavior, not minds.
- **Comparison to others.** No rankings. No percentiles. No "other learners at your level." Every learner is understood on their own terms.

## How Understanding Forms

AppleGo's understanding of the learner is not static. It deepens over time through three layers.

### Layer 1: Observation

Raw signals from learning behavior.

- "Paused at this PDF section for 4 minutes."
- "Drew this before attempting this problem."
- "Asked three follow-up questions about this concept."
- "Completed this practice in 8 minutes."

### Layer 2: Pattern

Recurring observations become patterns.

- "Always pauses longer on formal notation."
- "Consistently draws before deriving."
- "Sessions peak at 30-45 minutes."
- "Asks deeper questions when learning through projects."

### Layer 3: Understanding

Patterns become a coherent picture of the learner.

- "Thinks visually. Formal notation is a tool, not a starting point."
- "Self-directed. Sets own pace. Responds well to gentle guidance."
- "Resilient. Confusion does not discourage. It fuels deeper exploration."

This understanding is always provisional. The companion updates it continuously. A learner is not a fixed type.

---

## Learner Agency

The learner is always in control.

- The learner chooses what to learn.
- The learner chooses when to start and stop.
- The learner chooses which suggestions to accept.
- The learner chooses how to learn — reading, drawing, discussing, building.

AppleGo does not direct. AppleGo supports. The Environment adapts to the learner, not the other way around.

---

## Long-Term Relationship

Understanding deepens with time.

| Phase | What AppleGo knows |
|-------|-------------------|
| Day 1 | Nothing. Watches. |
| Day 7 | First patterns. Pace and style emerging. |
| Day 30 | Clear picture of learning style. Anticipates needs. |
| Day 90 | Knows when the learner is about to break through — or about to need rest. |
| Day 365 | Knows the full story. Can tell the learner who they've become. |

This deepening relationship is the core value of accumulated time.
