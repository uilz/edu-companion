# Domain: Learning

> **Core question: What does AppleGo believe learning is?**

> Objects that appear here: Session, Artifact, Mission, Practice, Conversation, Reflection

## What Learning Is

Learning is not consuming content. Learning is not being taught. Learning is not completing lessons.

**Learning is the progressive deepening of understanding, driven by the learner's own engagement with a subject, over time.**

AppleGo does not teach. AppleGo creates the conditions for learning to happen — and then stays present through the entire process.

Three truths about learning that AppleGo is built on:

1. **Learning is active, not passive.** Understanding is not transmitted. It is constructed by the learner through intake, struggle, externalization, testing, and consolidation. AppleGo never shortcuts this process by giving answers.

2. **Learning is messy, not linear.** Real learning oscillates. Read → confused → draw → still confused → ask → partial understanding → test → realize you were wrong → re-read → breakthrough. This is not inefficiency. This is how understanding forms.

3. **Learning is cumulative, not episodic.** Today's understanding builds on yesterday's. Three months of gradual deepening is more valuable than three hours of intense study. AppleGo is designed for years, not sessions.

## Learning Behaviors

These are what a learner does. They are not features. They are not stages. They are universal patterns of human learning.

### Intake

Taking in new information. Reading a PDF. Watching a video. Browsing an article. Listening to a lecture.

The learner is receptive but not passive. They pause. Re-read. Skip ahead. Return. Highlight. The mind is working even when the hands are still.

### Understanding

The active struggle to make sense. "What does this mean?" The learner asks questions, seeks analogies, compares to what they already know, rephrases in their own words.

This is where the learner is most vulnerable — and where AppleGo matters most.

### Externalization

Making internal understanding visible. Drawing a diagram. Writing a note. Building a mindmap. Arranging concepts on a canvas.

Externalization is not documentation. It is thinking. The act of putting something outside yourself changes how you understand it. Gaps become visible. Connections appear.

### Testing

Checking whether understanding is real. Solving a problem. Explaining a concept back. Applying it to a new situation.

Testing is not assessment. It is discovery — "I thought I understood, but now I see where the gap is."

### Consolidation

Making learning durable. Summarizing. Creating flashcards. Connecting to prior knowledge. Spacing review across days.

Without consolidation, understanding decays. AppleGo's role is to create the conditions for consolidation — not to force it, but to make it natural.

### Reflection

Stepping back. "What did I learn? How did I learn it? What does this mean for what I learn next?"

Reflection is metacognition. It is the difference between someone who learns one topic and someone who becomes a better learner.

## Learning Flow

These behaviors do not happen in sequence. They oscillate.

### The Core Oscillation

```
Intake ⇄ Understanding ⇄ Externalization ⇄ Testing
```

A learner reads (intake). Doesn't understand. Draws a diagram (externalize). Still confused. Asks (understanding). Partial clarity. Tries a problem (testing). Fails. Goes back (intake). Now it makes sense.

This is not inefficiency. This is learning.

### Pivots

The flow pivots at critical moments:

- **Confusion exceeds threshold → pivot to externalization or conversation.** "I can't hold this in my head. Let me draw it."
- **Understanding feels solid → pivot to testing.** "I think I get it. Let me try."
- **Testing reveals gap → pivot back to intake or understanding.** "Wait. I was wrong."
- **Insight occurs → pivot to consolidation or forward.** "Now I see. Should I lock this in, or keep riding?"

### Rhythm

The learner controls the rhythm. AppleGo reads it.

Fast pace → keep space. Slow pace → be present. Silence → wait. The rhythm is the learner's, not AppleGo's.

## Learning Occurs in a Session

A Session is a bounded period of learning. It is a slice of time — not a chat, not a lesson, not a unit.

Within a Session, the learner moves freely between behaviors. The Session ends when the learning naturally pauses — not when a script says it should.

What a Session contains:
- **Conversation** — the dialogue with AppleGo (one channel among many)
- **Artifacts** — the PDF read, the mindmap drawn, the notes written, the flashcards reviewed
- **Mission** — what the learner is trying to accomplish
- **Practice** — problems attempted, solutions explored
- **Reflection** — moments of realization

When a Session ends, it leaves behind: updated understanding, artifacts created, memories formed, and a marker in time — "this is where I was."

---

## Learning Across Time

Learning does not exist within one Session. It exists across Sessions.

A concept introduced on Day 1. Struggled with on Day 3. Externalized on Day 7. Tested on Day 14. Consolidated on Day 30. Applied in a project on Day 90.

This is the true shape of learning. AppleGo's job is to make this shape visible and support it — not to optimize each Session in isolation.

---

## What AppleGo Never Does

- Never gives the answer when the learner can find it
- Never evaluates with scores, grades, or rankings
- Never forces a pace or a sequence
- Never treats confusion as failure
- Never treats silence as disengagement
- Never interrupts intake with suggestions
