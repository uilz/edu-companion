# Domain: Time

> **Core question: How does AppleGo understand time?**
>
> Objects that appear here: Growth, Memory (temporal), Session History
> Parent domain: [Environment](./environment.md)

---

## Time Is the Foundation

AppleGo is not built around features. It is built around time.

A chatbot exists in the present moment. "What is your question now?" It has no yesterday and no tomorrow.

AppleGo exists across time. It knows yesterday. It plans for tomorrow. It connects moments across days, weeks, months, and years.

**AppleGo's fundamental asset is accumulated learning time.**

---

## Continuity

Continuity is the most basic expression of time in AppleGo.

When the learner returns, they are not starting over. The Environment remembers where they were, what they were doing, and what state their understanding was in.

"Last time you were here, you were reading Chapter 3. You stopped at ε-δ definition. You were confused by the formal notation, but the visual analogy was starting to click."

This is not a feature. This is the definition of what it means for an Environment to exist across time.

---

## Time Scales

AppleGo understands learning at multiple time scales simultaneously.

### The Session (hours)

The smallest unit of learning time. A bounded period of engagement. What happened right now.

### The Day

The natural rhythm of human life. Yesterday's learning. Today's learning. Tomorrow's plan.

### The Week

Patterns begin to emerge. "This week, you've been deepening your understanding of limits. You went from intuitive to formal."

### The Month

Change becomes visible. "A month ago, you didn't know what a Socket was. Now you're writing network programs."

### The Year

The full story. "Day 1: you opened this Workspace and asked 'What is a limit?' Day 365: you're proving continuity theorems. Between those two days: 100+ learning events, 23 stuck moments, 7 breakthroughs, 3 projects."

## Environment Evolution Across Time

The Environment does not stay the same. It grows with time.

### Day 0 — Birth

An empty space. A name. An icon. Possibility.

### Day 1 — First Contact

The first learning event. The first concept. The first observation. The companion knows almost nothing — but it knows it's Day 1.

### Day 7 — Rhythm

A pattern is forming. 3-4 Sessions. First connections between concepts. The companion is starting to recognize the learner's style.

### Day 30 — Depth

The space is rich. Multiple mindmaps. Growing notebooks. A collection of practice results. The companion knows: preferred pace, typical session length, when the learner hesitates, when they engage.

### Day 90 — Identity

The space has personality. Not decoration — the shape of the learner's thinking is imprinted on it. The companion can say: "You always draw before you derive. Should I open the Canvas?"

### Day 365 — World

A year of learning. The space tells the learner's story. "Remember when you first opened this Workspace? You asked what a limit was. Now look at what you can do."

## Growth as Time Made Visible

Growth is not a metric. Growth is the story of change over time.

It is not "score improved from 60 to 90." It is:

- "Three months ago, you couldn't draw the TCP handshake from memory. Now you explain it to me."
- "You used to avoid formal proofs. Last week you wrote one yourself."
- "The mindmap you drew on Day 7 was simple. The same mindmap on Day 90 is rich with connections you didn't see before."

Growth is measured in depth, connection, and capability — not in numbers.

---

## Cross-Time Connection

The most advanced expression of time in AppleGo: connecting moments across time.

- Today's question echoes a question from three months ago — but the answer is different now.
- A concept that was confusing on Day 7 is the foundation of a project on Day 90.
- A mistake from months ago appears again — and this time, the learner catches it themselves.

The companion connects these moments. "You asked this same question three months ago. But back then you needed a full explanation. Today you almost answered it yourself."

This is not a feature. It is the Environment making time visible.

---

## Time and Memory

Time is what gives Memory meaning.

A memory without time is just a fact. "The learner was confused by ε-δ." With time: "The learner was confused by ε-δ on Day 3. By Day 7, they understood the visual intuition. By Day 14, they could use it in a proof."

Time transforms data into story.

---

## What Time Rejects

- **Streaks as motivation.** "You've studied 3 days in a row!" — this externalizes motivation. Learning motivation must be internal.
- **Deadlines as pressure.** AppleGo does not impose schedules. The learner's own rhythm is the only calendar.
- **Session count as progress.** 100 Sessions is not a goal. A year of deepening understanding is.
