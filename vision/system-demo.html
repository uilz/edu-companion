Environment

What kind of space does learning happen in?

AI-Native Learning Environment. Not an App.
Moat = accumulated learning time, not features. ChatGPT can add PDF/mindmap in months.
It cannot replicate three years of history, evolved structures, and a companion that knows you.
P1: Remembers
Not files. Learning. Every event, understanding shift, path taken."上次你在数学基础，停在了 ε-δ"
P2: Evolves
Self-organizes. Concepts cluster. Adapts to learner style.TCP+HTTP+Socket → "Network" cluster emerges
P3: Protects Learning
Silence during intake. Presence during struggle. Space for mistakes.AI silent when reading. No scores. No streaks.
P4: Connects Across Time
Not just concepts. Moments. Day 1 → Day 365. The full story."三个月前卡在这里，现在一眼就看懂了"

Learning

What does AppleGo believe learning is?

Learning is the progressive deepening of understanding, driven by engagement, over time.
Not consuming content. Not being taught. Not completing lessons.
6 Learning Behaviors
IntakeReading, watching. Mind working even when hands are still.
UnderstandingActive struggle. "What does this mean?" Most vulnerable moment.
ExternalizationDrawing, writing, mindmapping. Making thinking visible.
TestingSolving, explaining back. Discovery — not assessment.
ConsolidationSummarizing, flashcards. Making learning durable.
ReflectionStepping back. "What did I learn? How?"
Core Oscillation
Intake ⇄ Understanding ⇄ Externalization ⇄ Testing
No fixed sequence. Driven by confusion and insight.
Objects That Appear Here
SessionBounded learning event. Not a chat. A slice of learning time.
MissionCo-produced direction. What the learner is trying to accomplish.
ArtifactPDF, mindmap, notes — learning materials. Persist after Session.

Companion

Who is AppleGo's AI?

Not a teacher. Not a tutor. Not a chatbot.
A companion who participates in the learner's entire learning life — through confusion and clarity, silence and conversation, Day 1 and Day 365.
6 Responsibilities
ObserveWhat is the learner doing? Where is attention?
SenseRead rhythm. Flow → space. Hesitation → ready. Silence → wait.
AdaptFormal not working? Try visual. Deep questions? Go deeper.
OrchestrateCoordinate artifacts. "要不要打开 PDF 上次的地方？"What makes AppleGo ≠ chatbot
RememberNot "scored 80%." But "hesitated on formal, flowed through visual."
GuideQuiet nudge. "上次卡在这里。继续，还是换条路？"
When Silent
Reading. Drawing. Writing. Coding. Thinking. In flow.
Silence is not absence. It is respect for learning.
What Companion Never Does
Gives answers. Interrupts intake. Evaluates with scores. Pushes when rest needed.

Time

How does AppleGo understand time?

AppleGo's fundamental asset is accumulated learning time.
A chatbot exists in the present. AppleGo exists across yesterday, today, and tomorrow.
Time Scales
SessionSmallest unit. What happened right now.
DayNatural rhythm. Yesterday → Today → Tomorrow.
WeekPatterns emerge. "This week: limits from intuitive to formal."
MonthChange visible. "A month ago you didn't know sockets."
YearFull story. 100+ events, 23 stuck moments, 7 breakthroughs.
Environment Evolution
Day 0Empty space. Name. Icon. Possibility.
Day 7Rhythm forms. First concept connections.
Day 30Rich. Multiple mindmaps. Companion knows style and pace.
Day 90Identity. Space reflects learner's thinking shape.
Day 365World. A year of learning. Space tells the story.
Objects That Appear Here
GrowthTime made visible. "You used to avoid proofs. Last week you wrote one."

Learner

How does AppleGo understand the learner?

Understanding = accumulated observation, not a profile form.
Patterns emerge over weeks. The learner may not know themselves.
What AppleGo Knows
StyleVisual before formal. Draws first. Learns by building.
Pace30-45 min sessions. Deep focus. Self-directed.
CurrentEnergy: engaged. Understanding: building. Emotion: curious.
What AppleGo Never Tracks
Demographics. Grades. Scores. Rankings. Private thoughts.
Depth Over Time
Day 1Nothing. Watches.
Day 30Clear picture. Anticipates needs.
Day 90Knows when breakthrough is coming.
Day 365Can tell the learner who they've become.

Memory

What is worth remembering for years?

Memory = the Environment's capacity to remember what happened.
Not a database. Not chat history. What is meaningful for future learning.
What Enters Memory
EventsRead Ch3. Drew mindmap. Solved 5 problems. Built server.
Understandingε-δ intuitive Day 3 → formal Day 14.
MomentsBreakthroughs. Connections made. Struggles before clarity.
PatternsPrefers visual. 30-45 min sessions. Energy peaks mid.
PathwaysDay 1 read → Day 3 draw → Day 7 try → Day 14 understood.
What Does Not Enter
Trivial exchanges. Raw logs. Temporary states. External scores.
Three Tiers
SessionWhat happened in this event. Hot. Active.
WorkspaceAll Sessions in one subject. Knowledge state. Artifact history.
GlobalAcross all Workspaces. Knows the learner — not just one subject.

Knowledge

What is knowledge in AppleGo?

Knowledge is the structured understanding built through learning.
Not facts. Not curriculum. Has shape. Depth. Connections. Evolves.
Structure
ConceptsLimit. Derivative. TCP handshake. The basic unit.
ConnectionsDerivative builds on limits. TCP/UDP are alternatives.
ClustersSelf-organized. TCP+HTTP+Socket → "Network."Environment recognizes patterns
DepthSurface → Understand → Apply → Connect → Create.
Knowledge vs Memory
KnowledgeWhat you understand. Structure. Concepts and connections.
MemoryHow and when you learned. Narrative. Events and patterns.
Evolution
WideningNew concepts added.
Deepening"I know the definition" → "I can prove theorems."
ReorgConnections shift. Isolated → cluster member.
CorrectionMisunderstandings resolved. Corrections are milestones.
