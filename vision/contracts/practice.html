Contract 04 · Practice

Self-testing. Not evaluation. The moment learning is proven or exposed.

1. Intent

—Learner tests understanding. Not to get a score — to discover what they actually know vs what they think they know. Wrong answers are the most valuable learning moments.

2. Invariants

I1Practice belongs to a Workspace. Scoped to one workspace's knowledge.
I2Generated from Knowledge state. Questions target weakest concepts — the gap between claimed and actual understanding.
I3Attempts are immutable. Every answer preserved. Append-only. Never overwritten.
I4AI reviews Attempt, never reveals the correct answer. "你的思路到这里是对的，但是..." — not "答案是 B。"
I5Attempt results update Knowledge. Correct → depth increases. Wrong → depth unchanged, struggle recorded.
I6Growth observes Attempts. Correct rate over time, concept-level progress, breakthrough moments.
I7Questions carry context. Linked to the Resources and Conversation turns that covered the concept.

3. Lifecycle

Created→Active→Reviewing→Completed
Created → Active: Questions generated from knowledge gaps. Presented to learner.
Active → Reviewing: Answer submitted. Attempt stored (immutable). AI evaluates.
Reviewing → Active: More questions remain. Next question presented.
Reviewing → Completed: All questions done. Summary compiled. Knowledge updated.
Publishes: PracticeStarted | AttemptSubmitted | AnswerReviewed | BreakthroughDetected | PracticeCompleted

4. Ownership

→Owned by: Workspace
→Owns: Question Attempt
→References: Knowledge.Concept Resource Conversation.Turn
→Observed by: Growth

5. Consistency

→Immediate: Question creation. Attempt submission.
→Async: AI review. Knowledge depth update. Growth milestone detection. Breakthrough moment recording.
